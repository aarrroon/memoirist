# Photo credits

The design explorations use real photos so people judge the designs rather than placeholder drawings. The journal around them is invented: these are not the photographers' journals, and nobody pictured is part of the story. Every photo comes from Wikimedia Commons under a licence that allows reuse with credit. `credits.json` is the machine-readable record, kept by the taste skill's `assets.mjs`.
