12Starlight, refined

The journal that won the last round, redrawn for the phone: photos edge to edge that you swipe, a clear line between entries, black, white and gold, and a Loot Drop that is a star whose light is still on its way to you.

Four quick questions

  1. Would you open this at 11pm to write about your day?
  2. Does the star make you want to seal something for next year?
  3. Light or dark?
  4. What would you change?

12Reading your journal

The feed, one entry, a week, every entry at once, an Era and its weeks, and a Loot Drop before and after it opens.

Good

  • Photos fill the phone and swipe, with a hairline and space between entries.
  • The first photo starts a quarter of the way down: the moon line, On this day and one line for the Era and the week sit above it.
  • The star shows how much of the wait has passed, and the date is said once.

Watch out

  • A wait that has just started barely lights the trail: the countdown does the talking for the first weeks.
  • The Era's black title band is the heaviest thing in the app. It might want to be a grey.
  • Long Era and week names share one line and wrap to two.

12At night

Dark mode is designed on its own: charcoal rather than black, off-white text and a softer gold. It follows your phone's setting unless you choose.
