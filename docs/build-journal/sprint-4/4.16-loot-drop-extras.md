## 5. How it was built
Read in this order.

1. **Date arithmetic**, [`frontend/src/lib/time.ts:266`](../../../frontend/src/lib/time.ts#L266) `addMonthsClamped(day, months)`: works on the ISO string's parts. `year * 12 + (month - 1) + months` turns the date into a month count, `Math.floor(index / 12)` and `index % 12` turn it back, and `Math.min(date, daysInMonth(...))` clamps. `daysInMonth` uses `new Date(Date.UTC(year, month, 0))`: day 0 of the next month is the last day of this one. No zone is involved, so a browser in New York gives the same answer.
2. [`time.ts:282`](../../../frontend/src/lib/time.ts#L282) `nextBirthday` is a practice block (section 11). [`time.ts:306`](../../../frontend/src/lib/time.ts#L306) `quickDays(today, birthday, earliest, latest)` builds the list and drops any day outside what can be picked. That one filter is how *Extend the seal* offers only later days (D-10): its `earliest` is the day after the current opening.
3. **The seal panel**, [`frontend/src/features/lootdrop/SealForm.tsx:78`](../../../frontend/src/features/lootdrop/SealForm.tsx#L78): `choice` is `'month' | 'year' | 'birthday' | 'pick' | null`, and the day is derived from it on every render (not stored), so a panel left open past midnight moves with the day. `RadioRow` ([`SealForm.tsx:252`](../../../frontend/src/features/lootdrop/SealForm.tsx#L252)) is a `<label>` holding a native radio (`sr-only peer`), the ring, and the label over its day. *Pick a date* reveals the day chip and calendar from task 4.8 ([`SealForm.tsx:105`](../../../frontend/src/features/lootdrop/SealForm.tsx#L105)). The rest (the "Opens" line, *Seal it*, the question) is unchanged.
4. **The birthday**: `LootDropPage` and `CaptureForm` now take `birthday` (from `useMe()` in `EntryPage` and `CaptureView`) and pass it to the form.
5. **The UI store**, [`frontend/src/stores/ui.ts`](../../../frontend/src/stores/ui.ts): `CaptureKind` gains `'letter'`; `replyTo` is set by `openCapture(kind, { replyTo })` and cleared by every other open and by close, so the Write menu never inherits a stale target. `CaptureHost` passes it down; `?write=letter` opens the letter.
6. **Capture**, [`frontend/src/features/capture/CaptureForm.tsx:156`](../../../frontend/src/features/capture/CaptureForm.tsx#L156): `kept.replyToEntryId` (the field 4.6 kept for exactly this) now starts from the store's `replyTo`, else the draft's. It is saved in the draft (`draft.ts`, optional for older drafts) and sent on every save. After a save the Loot Drop's own query is invalidated ([`:474`](../../../frontend/src/features/capture/CaptureForm.tsx#L474)), so its page lists the new reply. `ReplyLink` ([`:937`](../../../frontend/src/features/capture/CaptureForm.tsx#L937)) shows "In reply to *Letter to future me*" with a Remove button; a `400 fieldErrors.replyToEntryId` becomes the app's own sentence ([`:977`](../../../frontend/src/features/capture/CaptureForm.tsx#L977)). For the letter ([`:603`](../../../frontend/src/features/capture/CaptureForm.tsx#L603)): the heading is "Letter to future you", a line says when it opens, the editor's hint is "Dear future me…", the add bar's Seal, the phone seal panel and the laptop seal box are left out, and submitting opens `SealConfirm` ([`:918`](../../../frontend/src/features/capture/CaptureForm.tsx#L918)) for a month from today, whose confirm runs the same save-then-seal mutation as every seal.
7. **The entry page**, [`frontend/src/features/lootdrop/EntryPage.tsx`](../../../frontend/src/features/lootdrop/EntryPage.tsx): an opened Loot Drop shows *Reply to past you* as the primary button beside a quiet Delete ([`:164`](../../../frontend/src/features/lootdrop/EntryPage.tsx#L164)), and `Replies` ([`:231`](../../../frontend/src/features/lootdrop/EntryPage.tsx#L231)) lists the replies as rows with a chevron, keeping 44 px for each one still loading. A reply shows `AnswersLink` ([`:203`](../../../frontend/src/features/lootdrop/EntryPage.tsx#L203)) under its date: the Loot Drop's display title as a link; "a Loot Drop, sealed again" when the server sends it redacted; nothing when it is gone (`404`). An effect ([`:90`](../../../frontend/src/features/lootdrop/EntryPage.tsx#L90)) hands focus back to the Reply button when capture closes.
8. **The hook**, [`frontend/src/api/lootDrops.ts:51`](../../../frontend/src/api/lootDrops.ts#L51) `useReplies(ids)`: `useQueries` with `combine`, returning `{ replies, pending }`. It drops any reply that comes back locked, so a sealed entry is never listed by name (D-10), even from a stale `replyIds`.
9. **The first letter**, [`frontend/src/features/feed/FeedStates.tsx:79`](../../../frontend/src/features/feed/FeedStates.tsx#L79) `FirstLetter`: the `--surface` card with `lg` corners, the opening day a month from today in the user's zone, *Write the letter* (secondary, with the star mark) and a quiet *Skip*. Skip writes the mark ([`frontend/src/lib/firstLetter.ts`](../../../frontend/src/lib/firstLetter.ts), wrapped in try/catch for a blocked storage) and moves focus to *Write your first entry*, since the pressed button is gone.
10. **The fake backend**, [`frontend/src/test/msw/capture.ts:116`](../../../frontend/src/test/msw/capture.ts#L116): `POST /entries` now checks `replyToEntryId` the way the server does (`404` for a missing target, `400` for one sealed or never sealed) and adds the reply to the target's `replyIds`.

Docs: `docs/TECH_STACK.md` §3's tree had a placeholder `entry/` folder ("SealDialog with suggested dates, reply to past you") that never existed; it now describes `lootdrop/` as built, the `?write=letter` address and `lib/firstLetter.ts`. No endpoint, table or decision changed, so API_CONTRACT, DATABASE_SCHEMA and DECISIONS are untouched.

## 8. What went wrong
1. **Typecheck after the seal form changed:** `EntryPage.tsx(68,36): error TS2741: Property 'birthday' is missing`. Expected: the new required prop had to be passed from the page, which has `me.data`. Fixed by passing `birthday={me.data.birthday}`.
2. **A shell edit lost text.** Updating TECH_STACK.md with a `node -e "…"` one-liner in double quotes let Bash run the backticks in `` `?write=letter` `` as a command (`?write=dump: command not found`), so the line read "task 4.16:  opens the first letter". Caught by reading the diff back, fixed with the Edit tool. CLAUDE.md says to use Edit and Write for exactly this reason.
3. **Focus fell to `<body>` after Escape on the reply sheet.** The Playwright run showed `Escape closed the reply sheet; focus on body`. Capture opens from the store, not from a Radix or Vaul Trigger, so nothing knew where to return focus. Fixed with an effect in `EntryPage` that refocuses *Reply to past you* when `capture` becomes `null` after a reply, and a test that goes red without it.
4. **Prettier** flagged three files in `npm run lint`; `prettier --write` fixed them, and the whole suite was run again afterwards (512 passed).
5. **Local dev data:** the sample journal already had a reply ("Dear past me") to its opened Loot Drop, so the reply created through the API for the screenshots was a duplicate. It was deleted and erased. The dev user's birthday was set to 1994-08-12 for the seal panel screenshot and set back to `null` afterwards.

Nothing else failed: the 17 new component tests passed on their first run, which is why each rule was then broken on purpose (section 7).

At close-out (after review 1):
6. **A removed reply link came back on restore** (review 1, minor). A restored draft whose entry already exists took `replyToEntryId` from the server, so a link the user had removed returned. Fixed in `CaptureForm.tsx` by copying the soundtrack pattern: the draft's own value wins, and the server's is used only when the draft has none (`undefined`, a pre-4.16 draft). The first version of the new test asserted `replyToEntryId: null` in the `PUT` body and failed, because `saveEntry` leaves a `null` link out of the body (an omitted field clears it on a `PUT`, D-26). The test now asserts the field is absent. Putting the old line back turned it red (`Tests 1 failed | 26 skipped (27)`); with the fix, `npm test` gives `Tests 513 passed (513)`, and lint, typecheck and build exit 0.
7. **Stale file:line links** (review 1, minor): the journal's links for `kept`, the focus-return effect and the Reply button were moved to `CaptureForm.tsx:156`, `EntryPage.tsx:90` and `EntryPage.tsx:164`, and the other `CaptureForm.tsx` links were moved by the lines the fix added.

## 10. Concepts to learn from this task
- **Clamping calendar arithmetic** (`time.ts:266`): JavaScript's `Date` rolls over (`new Date(2027, 1, 31)` is 3 March). Turning the date into a month index, adding, and clamping the day to `daysInMonth` gives the answer people expect. `new Date(Date.UTC(y, m, 0))` is "the last day of month m" because day 0 is the day before the 1st.
- **Comparing ISO days as strings** (`time.ts:282`): `"2026-08-12" > "2026-08-11"` is a correct date comparison because the format is fixed-width, most significant part first. It avoids `new Date("2026-08-12")`, which is midnight UTC and the 11th in a browser west of Greenwich.
- **Derived state, not stored state** (`SealForm.tsx:78`): the form stores the *choice* (`'year'`), and the day is computed from it each render. Storing the day would go stale at midnight, and two pieces of state could disagree.
- **A visually hidden native input plus a drawn control** (`SealForm.tsx:252`): `sr-only peer` keeps the radio for keyboards and screen readers; `peer-focus-visible:` and `peer-checked:` style its sibling. The mistake is `display: none`, which removes it from the keyboard and the accessibility tree.
- **`useQueries` with `combine`** (`lootDrops.ts:51`): many queries in parallel under their own keys, folded into one object for the component. `combine` is also the place to filter (here, locked entries).
- **Focus management for things opened from state** (`EntryPage.tsx:93`): Radix and Vaul return focus to their Trigger; a panel opened by setting a store value has no Trigger, so the opener must take focus back itself when the store says it closed.
- **Per-viewer convenience in `localStorage`, always in try/catch** (`firstLetter.ts`): it can throw in a private window, and the screen must still work.

## 12. At work
- **Anniversary and "next occurrence" dates** are everywhere in banking: the next interest date, a card's expiry month, a direct debit "on the 31st" in a 30-day month, a customer's birthday for a KYC refresh. A reviewer there asks the same questions this task answers in tests: what happens on the 31st, on 29 February, on the day itself, and does any of it depend on the server's or the browser's zone.
- **Links between records where one side can be hidden** (here, a reply and a sealed Loot Drop) are like a transaction that references an account the viewer isn't entitled to see. The rule is the same: show that a link exists only as far as the viewer may know it, never the hidden record's fields, and test the hidden case explicitly.
- **Quick choices that still need confirmation** are the pattern of "pay the statement balance / the minimum / another amount", each followed by a confirmation that repeats the amount and date. One code path for all choices (here, every choice becomes a day and goes through the same `SealConfirm`) is what keeps them consistent.
- **Focus after a modal** is an accessibility review item in any regulated UI (WCAG 2.4.3 Focus Order): a reviewer will open and close every dialog by keyboard.

<details><summary>Answers</summary>

1. `Date` rolls an impossible day over: 31 February becomes 3 March, which would skip a month. The month index plus clamping gives 28 February.
2. 12 August *next year*: a Loot Drop can't open today (F-06), so the next birthday is strictly after today.
3. A panel left open across midnight would keep yesterday's "in a month" day; and the day and the choice could disagree. Deriving the day each render keeps one source of truth.
4. `display: none` removes it from the tab order and the accessibility tree, so the arrow keys, Space and the screen reader's "radio, 2 of 4" would be lost. `sr-only` hides it visually only.
5. "In reply to a Loot Drop, sealed again", linking to its Loot Drop page. The guarantee is the server: `EntryMapper` sends no title for a sealed entry (D-10). The client only chooses not to invent one, and the practice test checks it.
6. The Loot Drop's page lists its replies from `replyIds`, which the server just changed. Without the invalidation, its cached copy (30-minute staleTime) would show the old list.
7. Phase 1 has no account or server field for it, and adding a table for a convenience is out of scope. The cost: another browser or a cleared storage offers it again (harmless, and only until the journal has an entry).
8. Its day (a month from today) is before the current opening, and a seal can only move later (D-10). `quickDays` drops every day before `earliest`, which is the day after the current opening.
