# memoirist
**Your life, written down as it happens.**

Journaling apps ask you to sit down and write. Memoirist asks you to post. Dump a messy thought, snap a photo, talk into your phone, and move on. It files everything into weeks and chapters for you, so one day you open the app and find the story of your life instead of a pile of notes.

It looks like Instagram. It's only for you.

## Why journals die, and why this one won't
Blank pages feel like homework. A heap of random notes is impossible to look back on. So most journals last a month.

Memoirist fixes both ends. **Capturing takes seconds.** **Tidying up is automatic.** **Looking back is the reward.**

## What you get

### Capture anything in seconds *(Phase 1, in progress)*
- **Write, snap, talk.** Messy text, photos and voice notes. No formatting, no pressure.
- **Photo dump.** Pick a batch of photos and they're filed into entries, one per day they were taken.
- **Your own templates.** Set up headings like *Trips* or *Spiralling*, or start from a ready-made one.
- **Your own tags.** *Work*, *Family*, whatever you need.
- **A soundtrack.** Pin a Spotify song to an entry, a week or a chapter.
- **Keep your paper diary.** Photograph a page and it's filed on the right day, in your own handwriting.

### Your life files itself *(Phase 1)*
- **A private feed.** A scrollable timeline of your own moments. Nobody else sees it.
- **Weeks, filed for you.** Tap a week for a quick summary of what happened.
- **Eras you name.** *Uni Days*, *The Year I Moved Abroad*. Entries gather into them by date, each with a cover photo, and they can overlap.
- **On this day.** Memories from the same date in past years.
- **A simple timeline** of every entry, and a **Recently deleted** list that keeps things for 30 days.

### Write to future you *(Phase 1, with more in Phase 2)*
- **Loot Drops.** Seal an entry until a date you pick. Spiralling? Starting something big? Open it later for an honest look at where you were, and how far you've come.
- **Quick unlock dates**, a heads-up when one opens, and a reply to past you.
- **A first letter** to future you the day you start, and a **yearly letter** after that.

### A biographer, not a chatbot *(Phase 4)*
- **Help me think.** Stuck? A few quick one-tap questions and suggested tags.
- **Weekly and chapter summaries** written when you close a week or an Era.
- **The people in your story.** Profiles for the friends and family who keep turning up, and a map of who was around in each chapter.
- **Search by feeling.** "When did I last feel like this?"
- **Handwriting to text,** and a weekly email that wraps up your week.
- **The promise:** it never starts a conversation, never edits your entries, and you can ignore it completely. It only runs after you say yes, every feature has its own switch, and it never reads a sealed Loot Drop.

### Memories with friends *(Phase 3)*
- **Private groups** built from the entries you choose to add. Take them back any time.
- **Seal a trip.** Everyone writes their side, then it all opens together on a date you pick.
- **Every perspective.** Four people, four camera rolls, one shared story.
- **Trips become Eras** in everyone's own timeline.
- No followers, no likes, no public profiles.

### On your phone, and yours to keep *(Phases 2 and 5)*
- **Install it like an app,** with an app lock, and use it from anywhere.
- **Write offline,** scan diary pages, dictate instead of typing, and put photos inside your writing.
- **Share a week or an entry** with a link that expires, or export a week as a PDF.
- **A printable scrapbook** of your story, and a notification when a Loot Drop opens.
- **Export or delete all your data** whenever you like.

### Looking back with you *(Phase 6, opt-in)*
- **Patterns, spotted.** "You've felt flat on most Sunday evenings this month," with the entries to prove it.
- **Reminders of how far you've come.** See how a past worry turned out.
- **Highlight reels** of a chapter, a year or a friendship.
- **Predictions.** Seal a yes-or-no bet with yourself and see how well you knew you.
- **Your year in review** each December, and **a gift of memories** for a friend's birthday.

## Why people will trust it
- **Private by default.** Nothing is shared unless you choose to share it.
- **AI is optional,** asks first, and stays out of the way.
- **Sealed means sealed.** Loot Drops stay locked, even from the AI.
- **Your data is yours.** Export it or delete it all.
