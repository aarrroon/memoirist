# memoirist

**Your life, written down as it happens.**

Memoirist is a private journal that feels as easy as posting on Instagram, but it's only for you. Dump a messy thought, snap a photo, record a voice note, and move on. Memoirist handles the organising, so your journal turns into the story of your life without any effort.

## Why?

Most of us start journals and abandon them. Blank pages feel like homework, and a pile of random notes is impossible to look back on. Memoirist keeps the capturing effortless and does the tidying up for you.

## What you can do

- **Capture anything, instantly.** Write a few messy lines, add photos, or talk into your phone. No formatting, no pressure.
- **Your own templates.** Make writing templates with your own subheadings, like *Trips*, *Spiralling* or *Going out*, and start an entry from one. The prompts become headings you can fill in, skip or change.
- **Keep your paper diary.** Prefer a pen? Photograph the page and memoirist files it on the right day, with your handwriting just as you wrote it.
- **Looks like a feed, stays private.** Your entries appear as a scrollable timeline of your own moments. Nobody else sees it unless you choose to share something.
- **Your weeks, filed automatically.** Every entry slots into its week on its own, so you can flip back to "that week in March" without sorting anything.
- **Name the chapters of your life.** Create your own "Eras", like *The Year I Moved Abroad*, *Uni Days* or *The Startup Chapter*, and watch your entries gather into them.
- **Leave notes for your future self.** Seal an entry as a **Loot Drop** and lock it until a date you choose. It stays hidden, even from you, until the day it opens: a message in a bottle from past-you.

## Coming later: a biographer that helps without getting in the way

The AI in memoirist is a biographer, not a chatbot. It never starts a conversation, it never changes your entries on its own, and you can ignore it completely.

- **Help me think.** Lost for words? After you pause, a "help me think" button appears. Tap it and you get a few quick two-choice questions, like "stress or excitement?", and suggested tags like *Work* or *Family*. One tap each. Nothing is sent anywhere until you tap the button.
- **A summary of every week.** Close a week and your biographer writes a short account of how it went, so you can see the story of your life taking shape.
- **Chapter summaries.** When an Era wraps up, memoirist writes its story: what happened, what mattered and who was there.
- **Your people, understood.** The friends and family who keep turning up in your story get their own profile: who they are to you, the moments you share and which chapters of your life they belong to. Add your own notes, rename anyone, or hide them.

The AI only runs after you've said yes, every feature has its own switch, and it never reads anything sealed as a Loot Drop.

## Coming later: your paper diary, read for you

Plenty of us still write by hand, and plenty of us have a shelf of old notebooks we'll never type up. Memoirist is built so they belong in your story too.

- **Tap to read a page.** Photograph a page, tap "read this page", and memoirist turns your handwriting into text you can search and fix. Your original page stays exactly as you wrote it.
- **Your date, found for you.** If you wrote the date at the top, memoirist offers to file the page on that day.
- **Scan mode.** Point your phone at a page and it straightens, crops and brightens it for you. Photograph page after page, even without a signal.
- **Paper and phone, one story.** Pages you wrote by hand sit in the same timeline as everything else, and the biographer can include them in your weekly story.

Pages are only read when you ask, and never while they're sealed as a Loot Drop.

## Coming later: memories with friends

Some memories belong to more than one person. Soon you'll be able to start a private group for a trip, a festival weekend or a year in a share house, and build the story together. Your own journal stays yours: you pick which entries to add, and you can take them back whenever you like.

- **Seal a trip for later.** Everyone writes their side of the trip, then it all stays locked until a date you choose together, like the one-year anniversary. You'll see who has added something, but not what. Then it all opens at once.
- **One trip, every perspective.** Four people, four camera rolls, four versions of what happened. The biographer weaves everyone's memories into a single story, and only uses entries from friends who say yes.
- **See who's really in your story.** The people in your journal can be linked to your actual friends, so you can see who was part of which chapter of your life.
- **Trips become chapters.** A shared trip drops into everyone's own timeline as its own Era, automatically.

It's built for small groups of close friends. There are no followers, likes or public profiles.

## Coming later: looking back with you

After a few months of entries, memoirist knows your story well enough to notice what you'd miss.

- **Patterns, spotted.** "You've felt flat on most Sunday evenings this month." Gentle observations drawn from your own entries, with the entries that back them up.
- **Reminders of how far you've come.** Worried about something? See what you wrote about a similar worry last year and how it turned out. Feeling stressed? Set today beside the last time you felt this way, and what got you through.
- **Highlight reels.** The best moments of a chapter, a year or a friendship, gathered in one place and ready to relive.
- **A nudge while you write (optional).** Turn it on and memoirist can quietly offer one sharp question, or a better way to say what you mean, only when it's really worth it. This one may become a paid extra.

Insights are always opt-in, they wait in their own inbox instead of interrupting you, and they never use anything sealed as a Loot Drop.

## Who is it for?

Anyone who wants to remember their life but doesn't want journaling to feel like a chore, people who still write on paper, and friends who want to remember a trip together.

## Status

Early days. Memoirist is being built in phases, and the first phase is the core journal: capturing entries and filing them into weeks and Eras. The AI features, reading handwriting and the friends features come later.
