# memoirist

**Your life, written down as it happens.**

Memoirist is a private journal that feels as easy as posting on Instagram, but it's only for you. Dump a messy thought, snap a photo, record a voice note, and move on. Memoirist handles the organising, so your journal turns into the story of your life without any effort.

## Why memoirist?

Most of us start journals and abandon them. Blank pages feel like homework, and a pile of random notes is impossible to look back on. Memoirist keeps the capturing effortless and does the tidying up for you.

It's for anyone who wants to remember their life without it feeling like a chore, for people who still write on paper, and for friends who want to remember a trip together.

## What you can do

Memoirist is being built in phases, so some of this is still on the way. The [Timeline](#timeline) shows what ships when.

- **Capture anything, instantly.** Write a few messy lines, add photos, or talk into your phone. No formatting, no pressure.
- **Your own templates.** Make writing templates with your own subheadings, like *Trips*, *Spiralling* or *Going out*, and start an entry from one. The prompts become headings you can fill in, skip or change.
- **Keep your paper diary.** Prefer a pen? Photograph the page and memoirist files it on the right day, with your handwriting just as you wrote it.
- **Looks like a feed, stays private.** Your entries appear as a scrollable timeline of your own moments. Nobody else sees it unless you choose to share something.
- **Name the chapters of your life.** Create your own "Eras", like *The Year I Moved Abroad*, *Uni Days* or *The Startup Chapter*, and watch your entries gather into them. Chapters can overlap, so a trip can sit inside a bigger chapter.
- **Write to future you.** Spiralling? Tell the you of next month what it feels like. Starting something big? Write down what you're hoping for. Seal it as a **Loot Drop** and it stays locked until the date you pick, then opens as an honest look back at where you were, and maybe how far you've come.
- **A biographer that reads along.** It helps when you're stuck, writes up your weeks and chapters, and keeps a profile of the people who keep turning up in your story. Never a chatbot, always optional.

## A biographer that helps without getting in the way

The AI in memoirist is a biographer, not a chatbot. It never starts a conversation, it never changes your entries on its own, and you can ignore it completely.

- **Help me think.** Lost for words? After you pause, a "help me think" button appears. Tap it and you get a few quick two-choice questions, like "stress or excitement?", and suggested tags like *Work* or *Family*. One tap each. Nothing is sent anywhere until you tap the button.
- **A summary of every week.** Your biographer writes a short account of how each week went, so you can see the story of your life taking shape.
- **Chapter summaries.** When an Era wraps up, memoirist writes its story: what happened, what mattered and who was there.
- **Your people, understood.** The friends and family who keep turning up in your story get their own profile: who they are to you, the moments you share and which chapters of your life they belong to. Add your own notes, rename anyone, or hide them.

The AI only runs after you've said yes, every feature has its own switch, and it never reads anything sealed as a Loot Drop.

## Your paper diary, typed up for you

Plenty of us still write by hand, and plenty of us have a shelf of old notebooks we'll never type up. Memoirist is built so they belong in your story too.

- **Turn handwriting into text.** Photograph a page and memoirist turns your handwriting into text you can search and fix. Your original page stays exactly as you wrote it.
- **Scan mode.** Point your phone at a page and it straightens, crops and brightens it for you.
- **Paper and phone, one story.** Pages you wrote by hand sit in the same timeline as everything else, and the biographer can include them in your weekly story.

## Memories with friends

Some memories belong to more than one person. Start a private group for a trip, a festival weekend or a year in a share house, and build the story together. Your own journal stays yours: you pick which entries to add, and you can take them back whenever you like.

- **Seal a trip for later.** Everyone writes their side of the trip, then it all stays locked until a date you choose together, like the one-year anniversary. You'll see who has added something, but not what. Then it all opens at once.
- **One trip, every perspective.** Your diary is full of stories worth telling. Share one with friends and let them add their side: four people, four camera rolls, four versions of what happened. The biographer can weave it all into a single story, using only entries from friends who say yes.
- **Trips become chapters.** A shared trip drops into everyone's own timeline as its own Era, even when it sits inside a longer chapter you already have.

It's built for small groups of close friends. There are no followers, likes or public profiles.

## Looking back with you

After a few months of entries, memoirist knows your story well enough to notice what you'd miss.

- **Patterns, spotted.** "You've felt flat on most Sunday evenings this month." Gentle observations drawn from your own entries, with the entries that back them up.
- **Reminders of how far you've come.** Worried about something? See what you wrote about a similar worry last year and how it turned out. Feeling stressed? Set today beside the last time you felt this way, and what got you through.
- **Highlight reels.** The best moments of a chapter, a year or a friendship, gathered in one place and ready to relive.
- **A nudge while you write (optional).** Turn it on and memoirist can quietly offer one sharp question, or a better way to say what you mean, only when it's really worth it. This one may become a paid extra.

Insights are always opt-in, they wait in their own inbox instead of interrupting you, and they never use anything sealed as a Loot Drop.

## Timeline

Memoirist is being built in phases, in this order. Each one builds on the one before.

### Phase 1: The core journal (in progress)

- Write, add photos and record voice notes
- Your own templates with custom subheadings
- Your own tags, like *Work* or *Family*, to sort entries your way
- Photos of paper diary pages, filed on the right day
- Weeks and Eras, filed automatically
- Tap a week for a quick summary of what happened, or scroll a simple timeline of every entry
- Loot Drops, a scrollable feed and your settings

### Phase 2: Online, with accounts

- Sign up and use memoirist from anywhere
- Share an entry or a week with a link that expires
- Export a week as a PDF, and export or delete all your data

### Phase 3: The AI biographer

- Help me think, and tag suggestions
- Weekly summaries and chapter (Era) summaries
- Profiles of the people in your story
- Search by feeling, like "when did I last feel like this?"
- Turn handwriting into text

### Phase 4: On your phone

- Install it like an app and write offline
- Scan mode for diary pages
- Talk instead of type, photos inside your writing, and a printable scrapbook

### Phase 5: Memories with friends

- Private groups and shared stories
- Seal a trip for later
- One trip, every perspective
- Trips become chapters

### Phase 6: Looking back with you

- Patterns, reminders of how far you've come, and highlight reels
- An optional nudge while you write

Phases 4 and 5 can swap places, and the details inside each phase may shift as we learn what people use.
